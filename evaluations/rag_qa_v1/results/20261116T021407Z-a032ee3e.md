## Method

- Retrieval labels use manually verified gold document and Chunk keys.
- Answer correctness is deterministic fact coverage against manually authored `expected_facts`.
- Groundedness requires a matched answer fact to also appear in a cited full Chunk.
- Citation validity is structural; citation support is semantic fact-to-source matching.
- Ambiguous or partial matches remain `pending_review` and are not silently counted as human-verified correct.
- No LLM-as-a-Judge result is used as ground truth.
